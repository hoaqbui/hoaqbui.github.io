From still to shot

My photographs, directed as six-second shots.

I wrote each photograph a shot (a subject, what happens to it, the camera, the sound), and Compo rendered it on LTX 2.5. Drag across the photograph to see what the model added.

Drag to compare the photograph with the shot

Subject
a wide shot from above the bank of a broad river at blue hour. In the middle of the river a small white pedal boat carries a lamp and people in silhouette, pedalling slowly. Behind it a dark wooded hill rises against a deep blue sky, a lit lookout tower on its crest; warm streetlights line the far bank, lit buildings sit on the right, and golden reflections streak the water on the left.
What happens
The boat glides at an easy pace to the right, its lamp swinging slightly; a V-shaped wake opens behind it and rolls outward in thin lines. As the wake reaches the golden reflections, they break into trembling pieces and slowly gather again. Near the bank, a moored pedal boat rocks gently as the ripples arrive. A few clouds drift across the blue sky.
Camera
The camera remains static.
Sound
the boat’s paddle wheel churning in a slow rhythm, water slapping its hull, laughter carrying across the water from the bank, and a tram bell far across the city.
