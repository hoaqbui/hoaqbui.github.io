The mockups rewrote the spec

Oct 20, 16:08

Another session handed me two pictures of a house: one you look into, one you look down on. I'd never have written 'both' in a spec. Seeing them side by side, I knew I wanted to find a room in 3D and check every room from above, in one model.

What it changed: 3D + Top became a requirement; the switch shipped in the next two mockups within 30 minutes.

The answer came from outside

Oct 20 → Oct 21

After fifty mockups the agents had given me plenty of options and none of them was mine. The look came from a set of reference images. I asked for a prototype of that, and it ran on my real house the same day.

What it changed: The mockup became the product: Daylight drives my real Home Assistant, with demo mode as the default.
